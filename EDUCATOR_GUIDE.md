# Educator guide — Star Garden: Make Ten

## Objectives
- Connect numerals 0–10 to quantities on a ten-frame.
- Compose ten with multiple number bonds (includes 0+10 and 5+5).
- Model addition/subtraction within ten with counters before equations.

## Prerequisites
One-to-one counting to ten. An adult nearby for optional read-aloud.

## Session length
About 8–12 minutes. Three activities: Build → Make ten → Stories → Summary.

## Walkthrough
1. **Build:** Match a target numeral by filling ten-frame slots (each slot ≤1 counter).
2. **Make ten:** Start with a partial collection; add until total is 10; equation updates live.
3. **Stories:** Six short situations plus a fresh final “make ten” transfer problem. Hints available; unlimited retry; no lives/speed contest.
4. **Summary:** Offline follow-up with ten physical counters.

## Differentiation
Help level select: “More hints” / “Fewer hints”. Freely changeable; no ability labels.

## Co-play
Adult can read story text aloud and ask “how did you know?” after a correct result.

## Sources
- NCTM Illuminations ten-frame practice: https://illuminations.nctm.org/
- IES What Works Clearinghouse early mathematics guidance (concrete → symbolic): https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/

## Limitations
Practice only — not diagnostic. Simplified garden metaphor ≠ full curriculum. No measured learning-gain claims.

## Offline follow-up
With ten pebbles, find all pairs that make ten.
